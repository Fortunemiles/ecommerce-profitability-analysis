# E-Commerce Profitability Analysis — Key Findings

## 1. Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates e-commerce sales performance, profitability, product performance, retailer performance, geographic markets, order methods, sales trends, and discounting patterns.

The analysis was conducted using MySQL on sales data covering the period from January 2015 to July 2018.

### Overall Performance

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales Records | 149,257 |
| Total Units Sold | 19,795,947 |
| Total Revenue | 1.25B |
| Total Cost | 723.78M |
| Total Profit | 527.73M |
| Profit Margin | 42.17% |
| Total Discount Value | 66.47M |
| Overall Discount Rate | 5.04% |

## 2. Product Performance

### Product Line Performance

Personal Accessories generated the highest revenue at approximately
410.32M and the highest total profit at approximately 172.22M.

Golf Equipment generated approximately 331.84M in revenue and
157.99M in profit, making it the second-highest product line by profit.

Camping Equipment generated approximately 334.80M in revenue and
124.38M in profit.

Mountaineering Equipment generated approximately 156.85M in revenue
and 62.51M in profit.

Outdoor Protection generated the lowest revenue at approximately
17.70M but achieved the highest profit margin at 60.07%.

| Product Line | Revenue | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Personal Accessories | 410.32M | 172.22M | 41.97% |
| Golf Equipment | 331.84M | 157.99M | 47.61% |
| Camping Equipment | 334.80M | 124.38M | 37.15% |
| Mountaineering Equipment | 156.85M | 62.51M | 39.85% |
| Outdoor Protection | 17.70M | 10.63M | 60.07% |

### Top Products by Profit

The top-performing products by total profit were dominated by the
Hailstorm brand, particularly within the Golf Equipment product line.

The Hailstorm Titanium Woods Set generated approximately 25.91M in
profit, while the Hailstorm Titanium Irons generated approximately
20.22M.

Other high-performing products included the Hailstorm Steel Woods Set,
Lady Hailstorm Titanium Woods Set, Star Lite, Inferno, Legend, Canyon
Mule Weekender Backpack, and Hailstorm Steel Irons.

### Key Product Insights

- Personal Accessories is the largest contributor to total profit.
- Golf Equipment combines strong revenue with a high profit margin of
  47.61%.
- Outdoor Protection has the highest profit margin at 60.07%, despite
  having the lowest revenue among the product lines.
- Hailstorm products are particularly strong profit contributors,
  suggesting that the brand and Golf Equipment category deserve
  particular attention in future sales strategies.

## 3. Retailer Performance

### Top Retailers

The analysis identified the highest-performing retailers based on total
profit generated.

Grand choix generated the highest profit among the analyzed retailers,
with approximately 29.05M in profit from 67.11M in revenue.

Chen Yu Enterprise Co. generated approximately 23.50M in profit, while
VIP Department Stores generated approximately 18.96M.

| Retailer | Country | Retailer Type | Revenue | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Grand choix | Switzerland | Department Store | 67.11M | 29.05M | 43.29% |
| Chen Yu Enterprise Co. | Korea | Department Store | 56.60M | 23.50M | 41.52% |
| VIP Department Stores | Canada | Department Store | 43.73M | 18.96M | 43.37% |
| Leisure Land | United Kingdom | Department Store | 39.53M | 15.53M | 39.28% |
| Hangzhou Superman Sports Goods Co., LTD. | China | Golf Shop | 30.17M | 14.20M | 47.05% |

### Retailer Type Performance

Department Stores generated the highest total revenue and profit,
generating approximately 433.75M in revenue and 183.12M in profit.

Golf Shops generated approximately 221.28M in revenue and 103.48M in
profit, while Outdoors Shops generated 324.20M in revenue and 132.29M
in profit.

Direct Marketing had the highest profit margin at 53.79%, despite
having the lowest revenue among the retailer types.

| Retailer Type | Retailers | Revenue | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Department Store | 38 | 433.75M | 183.12M | 42.22% |
| Outdoors Shop | 91 | 324.20M | 132.29M | 40.81% |
| Golf Shop | 33 | 221.28M | 103.48M | 46.76% |
| Sports Store | 78 | 148.84M | 62.50M | 41.99% |
| Eyewear Store | 24 | 53.63M | 24.44M | 45.58% |
| Warehouse Store | 13 | 68.99M | 21.46M | 31.10% |
| Direct Marketing | 12 | 0.83M | 0.45M | 53.79% |

### Key Retailer Insights

- Department Stores are the largest contributor to overall retailer
  revenue and profit.
- Golf Shops have a strong 46.76% profit margin, making them one of the
  most profitable retailer types by margin.
- Direct Marketing has the highest profit margin at 53.79%, but its
  contribution to total revenue and profit is relatively small.
- Warehouse Stores have the lowest profit margin at 31.10%, indicating
  weaker profitability compared with other retailer types.
- The largest individual retailer, Grand choix, generated approximately
  29.05M in profit.
