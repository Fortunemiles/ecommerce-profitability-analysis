### E-Commerce Profitability Dashboard

The final analysis was visualized in an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of revenue, profitability, product performance, order methods, discount impact, and geographical sales performance.

### Dashboard Components
- Revenue Trend – Shows how revenue changed over time.
- Profitability by Category – Compares profit margins across product categories.
- Order Method Performance – Compares revenue and profit margin across order methods.
- Discount Impact – Examines how different discount levels relate to profit margins.
- Top 10 Products – Highlights the products generating the highest revenue.
- Revenue by Country – Shows the geographical distribution of revenue.
- Key Performance Indicators – Displays total revenue, total profit, profit margin, and quantity sold.
